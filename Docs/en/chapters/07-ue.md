# 7. What is a UE?

## UE = User Equipment

In plain English:

> **the end-user gadget on the network — a phone, a tablet, or another 5G terminal.**

For example:

* a phone
* a tablet
* some 5G terminals

In 3GPP language, your phone is not usually called a phone. It is often called:

> UE

So the paper says:

> Both a BS and a UE may act as an A-IoT reader.

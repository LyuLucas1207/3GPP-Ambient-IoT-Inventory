# 71. What is \(P_{sl}\)?

## sl = sleep

$$
P_{sl}
$$

is:

> the sleep-state power consumption.

The paper:

$$
P_{sl}=0.1\mu W
$$

and:

$$
P_{sl}<P_{rx}
$$

so sleeping uses far less energy than keeping the receiver on.

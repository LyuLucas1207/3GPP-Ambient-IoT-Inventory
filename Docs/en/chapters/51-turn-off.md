# 51. What is the turn-off threshold?

The paper writes:

$$
E_{es}^{\mathrm{low}}
$$

That is:

> **the turn-off threshold.**

The paper sets:

$$
E_{es}^{\mathrm{low}}=0.5E_{es}^{\max}
$$

Device 1:

$$
E_{es}^{\max}=500nJ
$$

So:

$$
E_{es}^{\mathrm{low}}=250nJ
$$

---

Therefore:

```text
500 nJ
↑
Turn ON

work
work
work
↓
250 nJ
Turn OFF

charge
charge
charge
↑
500 nJ
Turn ON
```

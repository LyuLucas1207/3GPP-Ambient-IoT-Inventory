# 76. What is occupancy?

## Occupancy

It means:

> how full the AOs are.

For example, 8 AOs:

```text
AO1 occupied
AO2 collision
AO3 occupied
AO4 collision
AO5 occupied
AO6 empty
AO7 collision
AO8 occupied
```

From these outcomes the reader can estimate:

> whether things are too crowded right now.

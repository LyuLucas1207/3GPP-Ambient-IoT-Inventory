# 57. P3: keep colliding, retrying, and draining the energy

Suppose the device has energy.

But:

```text
Attempt 1 → collision
Attempt 2 → collision
Attempt 3 → collision
Attempt 4 → collision
```

Each attempt still needs:

* wake up
* receive
* transmit
* wait

so it keeps burning energy.

In the end:

```text
not successful
+
out of energy
```

That is P3.

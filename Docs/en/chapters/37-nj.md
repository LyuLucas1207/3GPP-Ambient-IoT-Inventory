# 37. What is an nJ?

## nJ = nanojoule

J = joule:

> The unit of energy.

nano:

$$
10^{-9}
$$

So:

$$
1nJ=10^{-9}J
$$

Device 1:

$$
500nJ
$$

That is truly very small.

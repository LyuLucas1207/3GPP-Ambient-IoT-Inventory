# 40. What is \(P_{tx}\)?

## Tx = Transmit

So:

$$
P_{tx}
$$

is:

> The device’s power consumption while sending a signal.

Device 1:

$$
1\mu W
$$

Device 2:

$$
200\mu W
$$

From now on, when you see:

* Rx = receive
* Tx = transmit

let it be a reflex.

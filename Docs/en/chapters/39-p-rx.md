# 39. What is \(P_{rx}\)?

## Rx = Receive

So:

$$
P_{rx}
$$

is:

> **The device’s power consumption while receiving a signal.**

Device 1:

$$
P_{rx}=1\mu W
$$

Device 2:

$$
P_{rx}=50\mu W
$$

# 45. What is \(\xi(p_{in})\)?

The Greek letter:

$$
\xi
$$

is pronounced:

> xi (roughly “ksai” or “zai”).

Here it means:

## Power Conversion Efficiency

> The efficiency of turning RF energy into usable electrical energy.

For example:

$$
\xi=5\%
$$

Meaning:

You receive:

```text
100 units RF energy
```

and what actually gets stored is:

```text
5 units
```

The rest is lost.

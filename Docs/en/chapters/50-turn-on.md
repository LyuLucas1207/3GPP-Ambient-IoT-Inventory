# 50. What is the turn-on threshold?

The paper writes:

$$
E_{es}^{\mathrm{up}}
$$

That is:

> **the turn-on threshold.**

For Device 1, for example:

$$
E_{es}^{\max}=500nJ
$$

The paper sets:

$$
E_{es}^{\mathrm{up}}=E_{es}^{\max}
$$

So:

> it must charge up near full before it turns on.

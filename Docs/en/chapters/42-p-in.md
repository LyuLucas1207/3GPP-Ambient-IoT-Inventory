# 42. What is \(p_{in}\)?

## in = input / incident

$$
p_{in}
$$

is:

> **The RF power that actually arrives at the tag.**

The reader may send:

> a strong signal

but the signal fades as it travels through the air.

So:

```text
close to the reader
→ pin is higher

far from the reader
→ pin is lower
```

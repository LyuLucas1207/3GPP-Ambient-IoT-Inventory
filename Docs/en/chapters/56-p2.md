# 56. P2: awake, but not enough energy to finish the procedure

This is more like:

> a phone with 1% battery.

It can still light up the screen.

But the moment you open a heavy game:

> it shuts off.

The tag is the same:

```text
received paging ✓

send Msg1 ✓

receive Msg2 ...

out of energy
```

Result:

> CBRA is not completed.

The paper defines this as P2.

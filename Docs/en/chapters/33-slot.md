# 33. What is a slot?

## Slot = time slot

It means chopping time into small boxes.

In the paper’s parameters:

$$
1\ slot=0.5ms
$$

arXiv Table I sets it this way.

---

## What is ms?

### ms = millisecond

A millisecond.

$$
1ms=0.001s
$$

So:

$$
0.5ms=0.0005s
$$

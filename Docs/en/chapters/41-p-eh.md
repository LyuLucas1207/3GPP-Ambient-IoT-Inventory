# 41. What is \(P_{eh}\)?

## EH = Energy Harvesting

So:

$$
P_{eh}
$$

is:

> **The power at which the device collects energy.**

The paper defines:

$$
P_{eh}=p_{in}\xi(p_{in})
$$

We will unpack this one piece at a time.
